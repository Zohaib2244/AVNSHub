clock & date — redesign directions
Eight directions, each with real S / M / L content (not the same markup shrunk down). A-D are the plainer first pass; E-H push further with more illustration and personality. Pick a letter — or call out pieces from a couple of them ("E's sky but G's log line") — and it gets built as the real widget.
A Minimal Numerals
Strip it back — the time is the only hero at every size. S is just two lines. M adds a barely-there day-progress thread (no bar chrome, just a line and a dot). L is the first place sunrise/sunset, lofi, and full date show up at all — as a quiet side column, not stacked rows.

H Living Dot-Matrix
Reuses the dot-matrix language already living in this app (glyph matrix / dot matrix widgets) instead of introducing a new visual vocabulary — a softly pulsing dot field standing in for the sun, with the lit cluster's position and spread tracking time-of-day. The calendar at L is drawn the same way instead of a plain grid, so the whole card reads as one consistent idea.

D2 Split Card — Sky Strip
Same two-zone bones you liked, but the day-at-a-glance strip stops being a flat gradient rectangle and becomes an actual illustrated sky slice — E's sky merged into D's shape. A couple of stars twinkle in the night end, a tiny skyline sits at the base, and the sun genuinely travels the strip on a gentle curved path instead of sliding straight up and down. Even S's thin accent spine gets the gradient + a star, so the idea survives at every size instead of disappearing until M.
