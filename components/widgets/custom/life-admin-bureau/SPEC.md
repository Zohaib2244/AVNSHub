## Concept

A mock municipal bureaucracy widget that treats ordinary personal-life friction as official paperwork. It generates fake notices from departments like Unanswered Messages, Laundry Permits, Hydration Compliance, Sleep Debt Recovery, and Snack Zoning.

## Per-size content

- **S**: one bureaucratic status, e.g. 3 forms overdue
- **M**: today's fake life-admin case file with department, violation, deadline, penalty, and absurd remediation step
- **L**: a full personal bureaucracy panel with open cases, fake departments, stamped approvals, escalating notices, and a pointless compliance history

## Additional notes

Tone should feel petty, procedural, and overly formal. Good interactions include stamp as resolved, appeal decision, request extension, and generate form. Resolving one case can create a follow-up notice so the bureaucracy never truly ends.
